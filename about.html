About IronForge

FORGED IN LAHORE, RUN ON COACHING.

Since 2016 we've built one of the city's most respected training floors — and the community that fills it every single day.

Our story

FROM A SINGLE RACK TO 10,000 SQ FT.

IronForge started in 2016 as a small strength room in Gulberg III — a few benches, a single squat rack and a coach who believed training should be coached, not just opened. Word spread, and so did the floor.

Today, IronForge spans 10,000 sq ft across the full training floor, a dedicated women's zone, a functional turf, a cardio deck and a recovery space. What hasn't changed is the rule the gym was built on: technique first, ego never, community always.

  • 15+ certified coaches across all disciplines
  • 500+ active members and growing
