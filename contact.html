Contact FAQ

QUICK ANSWERS.

Can I visit before joining?

Absolutely. Book a free tour and trial session on WhatsApp — walk the floor, meet a coach, train with us once before you decide.

Is there a registration or joining fee?

No. Membership starts at PKR 8,500/month with nothing extra to pay at sign-up. The fitness assessment and onboarding session are included.

Do you have a women's-only space?

Yes — a fully equipped women's floor with female coaches and dedicated class slots every day. You can also train there 24/7 with an active plan.

What should I bring on my first day?

Comfortable training clothes, training shoes and a water bottle. Everything else — from chalk to the welcome kit — is on us.
